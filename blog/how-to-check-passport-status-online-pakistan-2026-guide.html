How to Check Your Passport Status Online in Pakistan (2026 Guide)

By Anwaar Digital · Last updated: 28 November 2026 · Travel & government services

Applied for a passport? Here is how to track it

You submitted your passport application, paid the fee and received a receipt — and now you are waiting. The good news is that you do not need to visit the passport office again and again to ask where your passport is. The Directorate General of Immigration & Passports (DGIP) lets you check your passport status online, using the tracking number printed on your fee receipt.

This guide explains every official way to check your passport status in Pakistan: the DGIP website tracking page, the online passport portal account, the helpline, and what to do in person if the online status does not move. It also explains what each status message means, how long each category usually takes, and how to avoid the agents and fake websites that target passport applicants.

Before you start, keep two things ready: your tracking number (also called the tracking ID, printed on your fee receipt) and your CNIC number. Without the tracking number, the online check will not work — so find your receipt first. If you applied online through the passport portal, your login email and password are enough instead.

What you need before you check

  • Tracking number: the number printed on the fee receipt you received when you submitted the application at the passport office. Keep the receipt safe until the passport is in your hand.
  • CNIC number: your 13-digit Computerised National Identity Card number. Some checks ask for it to confirm the application belongs to you.
  • Online application login: if you applied through the DGIP online portal, the email address and password you registered with.
  • A working phone number: the mobile number you gave on the application. Status SMS updates, if any, go to this number, so keep the SIM active.

Never share your tracking number or CNIC number on social media or with strangers. Anyone who has your tracking number can see your application status, and your CNIC number is sensitive personal information. Share it only on the official DGIP website or with the passport office itself. Method 1: Check on the DGIP website (fastest)

The official DGIP website has a passport tracking page. This is the quickest check and works from any phone or computer with internet.

  1. Open the official DGIP website at dgip.gov.pk. Type the address yourself — do not click links sent to you by strangers, because fake passport websites are common.
  2. Look for the tracking or track your passport option on the homepage or in the services menu.
  3. Enter your tracking number exactly as printed on your receipt, without spaces or dashes.
  4. Enter your CNIC number if the form asks for it, then submit the form.
  5. Your current application status appears on the screen — for example "application received", "in process", "printing" or "dispatched".

Write down the status and the date you checked it. If you check again in a few days and the status has not changed, that is normal for the first part of the process — printing and dispatch are the stages where the status moves most. Check once or twice a week rather than every day; the system updates as your file moves between stages, not in real time.

Method 2: Check through the online passport portal

If you applied online through the DGIP online MRP portal instead of visiting an office, your application lives in your portal account, and the status is shown there.

  1. Open the online passport portal (the same website where you created your application) and log in with your registered email and password.
  2. Go to your dashboard or the my applications section.
  3. Select your passport application to see its current status and any remarks.
  4. If the portal asks for more documents or a correction, it will say so here — follow the instructions exactly and upload what is asked.

Online applicants should check the portal inbox as well as the status, because the passport office may send a message asking for a document or a correction. A missed message is one of the most common reasons an online application stops moving. If you have forgotten your portal password, use the "forgot password" link on the login page — never create a second account, because two applications under one CNIC cause delays.

Method 3: Helpline and visiting the passport office

If the website shows no result, or the status has not changed for a long time, you have two more options.

Call the DGIP helpline

Call the DGIP helpline on 051-111-344-777 during working hours. Keep your tracking number and CNIC number ready before you call, because the operator will ask for them. The helpline can confirm your status and tell you if your file needs anything. If the line is busy, try again later in the day — mornings are usually the busiest time.

Visit the passport office

Visit the regional passport office where you submitted the application. Take your original CNIC and your fee receipt — without the receipt, the staff cannot trace your file quickly. Go to the inquiry or token counter and give them your tracking number. They can check the internal system, which sometimes shows more detail than the public website.

Do not pay anyone inside or outside the office who offers to "speed up" your passport for extra money. The official fee is the only fee. Paying an agent is not only a waste of money — it can put your application at risk if the agent submits false information.

What each passport status means

The tracking page shows short status messages. Here is what they normally mean:

StatusWhat it means
Application receivedYour application and fee have been accepted at the office. The file is in the queue.
In process / under processYour documents and biometrics are being checked. This is usually the longest stage.
PrintingYour passport is being printed. You are close to the end of the process.
DispatchedYour passport has been sent from the printing office towards your regional passport office or delivery address.
Ready for collection / deliveredYour passport has arrived. Collect it from the office with your original CNIC and receipt, or wait for delivery if you chose that option.
Objection / incompleteSomething is missing or wrong — a document, a photo, or a detail. Contact the office quickly to find out what is needed.

If your status shows an objection, do not ignore it. Visit the office or call the helpline the same week. Objections do not fix themselves, and the longer they wait, the longer your passport takes.

How long does a Pakistani passport take?

Pakistan offers passport processing in different categories — normal, urgent and fast-track — and each has its own fee and its own expected time. The receipt you received shows which category you applied under, and the expected delivery time is linked to that category, not to a fixed number of days for everyone.

  • Normal: the lowest fee and the longest wait. Suitable if you have no travel date soon.
  • Urgent: a higher fee and a shorter wait than normal. Suitable if you need to travel within weeks.
  • Fast-track: the highest fee and the shortest wait. Suitable when travel is very soon.

Real delivery times vary with workload, public holidays and the number of applications in the system. A passport applied for during a busy season can take longer than the same category in a quiet month. Your receipt and the tracking page are the only reliable sources for your own timeline — do not trust a date promised by an agent or a stranger. Plan travel only after the passport is in your hand, and check the live flights and the weather forecast for your destination once your travel date is fixed.

Common problems and how to fix them

The tracking page shows no record

First, re-type the tracking number carefully — one wrong digit gives no result. Check that you are on the official DGIP website and not a copy. If the application was submitted very recently, wait 24 to 48 hours: new applications can take a day or two to appear in the online system. If it still shows nothing after two days, call the helpline or visit the office with your receipt.

Status stuck on the same stage for weeks

Some stages, especially verification, take time. But if the status has not moved for several weeks — far beyond the expected time for your category — visit the passport office with your receipt and ask for the file to be checked. Bring your CNIC and a copy of the receipt. Ask for the reason in writing if the staff say there is a problem.

You lost your fee receipt

Go to the passport office where you applied with your original CNIC. The office can trace your application by CNIC and issue a duplicate receipt or give you the tracking number. This takes time, so keep the receipt safe from the start — take a clear photo of it on your phone as a backup on the day you apply.

Wrong information on the application

If you notice a wrong name spelling, wrong date of birth or wrong address after submitting, visit the passport office immediately and ask for a correction before printing. Fixing a mistake after the passport is printed means a new application and a new fee.

Passport dispatched but not received

If the status says dispatched and nothing arrives for several days, check with the regional passport office first, then with the courier or delivery service if one was used. Keep your tracking number ready — it is the key to tracing the parcel at every step. Our guide to live parcel tracking in Pakistan explains how delivery tracking works in general.

Stay safe: fake websites and agents

  • Use only dgip.gov.pk for passport tracking and information. Any other website asking for your tracking number or CNIC is not official.
  • Never pay through a link sent on WhatsApp or SMS. The official fee is paid at the bank or through the official channel printed on your receipt.
  • No one can guarantee same-day delivery from outside the passport office. Anyone who promises it for extra money is running a scam.
  • Keep your CNIC number private. Do not post your receipt, tracking number or CNIC on Facebook, WhatsApp groups or any public place.
  • Check before you travel. Once the passport arrives, check the name spelling, date of birth and expiry date on the spot. Also check the currency exchange rate and the gold rate before a trip abroad — small checks before travel save big problems later.

Fraudsters copy the look of government websites very well. If a page asks for payment by JazzCash, Easypaisa or a personal bank account for a "passport fee", close it immediately and report it. Frequently asked questions

Can I check my passport status without the tracking number?

Not online. The tracking page needs the tracking number from your fee receipt. If you lost the receipt, visit the passport office with your original CNIC — the staff can find your application and give you the tracking number again.

Can I check someone else's passport status?

Only if you have their tracking number and their permission — for example, a parent checking a child's application. Do not check or share another adult's passport status without their agreement.

Does the passport office send SMS updates?

Some applicants receive SMS updates on the mobile number given in the application, but this is not guaranteed for every application. Do not rely on SMS alone — check the DGIP website yourself once or twice a week.

What documents do I need to collect my passport?

Take your original CNIC and your fee receipt to the passport office. If someone else is collecting it for you, they usually need an authority letter and their own CNIC as well — confirm the exact rule with the office before sending anyone.

My passport is ready, but I cannot collect it for a month. Is that a problem?

Passports are normally held at the office for collection for a reasonable period, but do not leave it for months. Collect it as soon as you can, and check the office's holding rule if you will be away for a long time.

Can I renew my passport online?

Yes — the DGIP online portal allows online renewal for many applicants. You create an account, fill in the form, upload documents and pay online, then track the application in the same portal. First-time applicants usually still need to visit an office for biometrics.

The bottom line

Checking your passport status online in Pakistan takes two minutes: open dgip.gov.pk, enter the tracking number from your fee receipt, and read the status. If you applied online, log in to the passport portal instead. If the website shows no record or the status stays stuck for weeks, call the helpline on 051-111-344-777 or visit the passport office with your original CNIC and receipt.

Keep your receipt safe, never share your tracking number or CNIC publicly, and never pay an agent who promises to speed things up. Your passport is your most important travel document — treat the tracking number like a password.
